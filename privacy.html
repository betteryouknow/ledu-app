Ledu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Overview

Ledu ("the app", "we") is a personal-growth app. We designed Ledu to need as little of your data as possible. There is no account, no login, and no Ledu server. Your progress, reflections, and preferences are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us: your progress (XP, level, streaks), your written reflections, your selected growth path and goal, your reminder preferences, and your subscription status. Deleting the app removes this data.

AI-generated lessons (Google Gemini)

Daily lessons are generated by Google's Gemini API. To create a lesson, the app sends the growth path you chose, your goal, your experience level, and — if present — your most recent reflection text to Google's API. This content is used only to generate your lesson. We do not attach your name, email, or any account identifier, because the app has none. Google's handling of API data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information (such as health, financial, or identifying details) into reflections or goals.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data on any server.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Ledu is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Not medical advice

Ledu provides educational self-growth exercises only. It is not medical, mental-health, or therapy advice.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
