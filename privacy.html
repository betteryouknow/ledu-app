Ledu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overview

Ledu ("the app", "we") is a personal-growth app. We designed Ledu to need as little of your data as possible. There is no account and no login. Your progress, reflections, and preferences are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (XP, level, streaks), written reflections, growth path and goal, reminder preferences, and subscription status are stored on your device. Selected goal, path and reflection content may also be sent through our secure backend for the AI lessons described below. Deleting the app removes the locally stored copy.

AI-generated lessons (Google Gemini)

Daily lessons are generated by Google's Gemini API. To create a lesson, the app sends the growth path you chose, your goal, your experience level, and — if present — your most recent reflection text to Google's API. This content is used only to generate your lesson. We do not attach your name, email, or any account identifier, because the app has none. Google's handling of API data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information (such as health, financial, or identifying details) into reflections or goals.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data. Our backend does not save lesson request content or generated lesson responses; it retains the security and usage records described below.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Ledu is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Not medical advice

Ledu provides educational self-growth exercises only. It is not medical, mental-health, or therapy advice.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Secure AI processing and abuse prevention

In the security update, AI requests you choose to submit travel over HTTPS through our Cloudflare-hosted backend to Google Gemini. Our backend processes the submitted content and the generated response in memory without saving their contents.

To authenticate the app, prevent abuse and control AI spending, the backend retains an opaque App Attest public key identifier and public key, assertion counters, short-lived challenge and rate-limit records, and usage totals. IP addresses are processed for connection security; our application rate-limit records use hashed values. These security records are separate from the content stored on your device and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Public keys and counters are retained to verify future requests.

Cloudflare provides backend hosting and network security. Google provides AI processing. Their handling of data is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies. Deleting local app content removes the on-device copy; it does not automatically remove the separate backend security records.
