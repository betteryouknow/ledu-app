Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Ledu has no login. Your progress and reflections stay on your device.

Where do the daily lessons come from?
Each lesson is generated fresh by AI (Google Gemini) based on the growth path and goal you choose, so it's tailored to you.

What does Premium include?
Every growth path, your personal 7-day plan, weekly review and deeper insights, a saved lesson library, and bonus surprise challenges.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Ledu, and change or cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open Ledu → Settings → Restore Purchases.

A lesson won't generate / "AI is busy".
This usually means the AI service is briefly busy. Tap Try again in a moment — the app retries automatically and it typically clears quickly.

Is Ledu therapy?
No. Ledu offers educational self-growth exercises only. It is not medical, mental-health, or therapy advice.
